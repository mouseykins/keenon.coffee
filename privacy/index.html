Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Who we are

Keen On Coffee is a coffee video channel run by Martin Keen. This policy covers this website, keenon.coffee, and the Keen On Coffee uploader, a private tool Martin uses to publish videos to his own YouTube channel. Questions: martin@keenon.coffee.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no forms.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers. See Google's privacy policy.

Email

If you email martin@keenon.coffee, your message and address are used only to reply to you. They are not sold, shared or added to a mailing list.

The Keen On Coffee uploader

The uploader uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions to the Keen On Coffee YouTube channel, and to read back those videos' details to check they were saved correctly. It is used only by Martin Keen, on his own computer, for his own channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • What it accesses: the Keen On Coffee YouTube channel's identity and the videos it uploads or updates, through the YouTube scope you approve when signing in.
  • Where data is kept: the sign-in token is stored only on Martin's computer. There is no server and no database; nothing is sent anywhere except to YouTube.
  • Sharing: no Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes the stored token useless; deleting the token file removes it.

The uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
